Cozy Paint Sort — Privacy Policy

Effective 2026-10-14 · 한국어 보기

Cozy Paint Sort (the "App", package com.jluxena.paintsort; shown as "포근한 물감 정리" on Korean devices) is a color sorting puzzle game made by an independent developer. There is no sign-up, there is no developer server, and the developer does not collect or store your personal information.

1. What is stored on your device

Your current level, coins, finished paintings, records (moves and times) and your sound and vibration settings are stored only on your device. The developer cannot see them, and they are removed when you uninstall the App.

2. Advertising (Google AdMob)

The App shows Google AdMob ads at the following moments:

  • Full-screen (interstitial) ads — when you tap Next level after clearing a level and when you tap Restart on a level you have already started (both from level 6 on, and not more often than once every 40 seconds); and each time you tap Add jar (the ad is the price of the extra jar).
  • Rewarded ad — only when you choose it yourself: when you do not have enough coins and tap Hint, you receive the hint after watching.

There are no banner ads, and no ad appears by itself while you are pouring. To serve ads, Google may collect the following.

ItemPurpose
Advertising IDAd delivery, frequency capping, fraud prevention

See the Google Privacy Policy and the AdMob policies for details. You can reset your advertising ID or turn off personalised ads in your device settings. Where required, the App asks for your consent before requesting any ad.

3. Permissions

PermissionWhy
Internet, network stateTo show ads
Advertising IDUsed by the Google Mobile Ads SDK to serve and measure ads

The App does not use the camera, location, contacts, microphone, notifications, or your photos and files.

4. What the App never does

  • It does not require registration or login.
  • It does not collect your name, phone number, or email address.
  • It does not send your progress or records to any developer server.
  • It has no in-app purchases or subscriptions.

5. Children

The App contains nothing harmful to children. Ad content is capped at the parental-guidance (PG) rating. The App is not directed at children under 13 and does not knowingly collect information from them.

6. Changes

If this policy changes, the new version and its effective date are posted on this page.

포근한 물감 정리 (Cozy Paint Sort) 개인정보처리방침

시행일: 2026-10-14

포근한 물감 정리(영어 이름 Cozy Paint Sort, 이하 "앱", 패키지 이름 com.jluxena.paintsort)는 개인 개발자가 만든 색 물감 정리 퍼즐 게임입니다. 앱은 회원가입이 없고, 개발자 서버가 없으며, 이용자의 개인정보를 수집·보관하지 않습니다.

1. 기기 안에만 저장되는 것

지금 단계, 코인, 완성한 작품, 기록(움직임 횟수·걸린 시간), 소리·진동 설정은 모두 이용자의 기기 안에만 저장됩니다. 개발자는 이 정보를 볼 수 없고, 앱을 지우면 함께 사라집니다.

2. 광고 (Google AdMob)

앱은 다음 순간에 Google AdMob 광고를 보여 줍니다.

  • 화면 전체를 덮는 전면광고 — 단계를 깨고 다음 단계를 누를 때와, 이미 시작한 판에서 다시하기를 누를 때(둘 다 6단계부터, 40초에 한 번까지), 그리고 병 추가를 누를 때마다 (광고가 병 하나의 값입니다).
  • 보상형 광고 — 이용자가 직접 고를 때만 나옵니다: 코인이 모자랄 때 힌트를 누르면 광고를 본 뒤 힌트를 받습니다.

배너 광고는 없고, 물감을 붓는 도중에 저절로 뜨는 광고도 없습니다. 광고 게재를 위해 Google 이 아래 정보를 수집할 수 있습니다.
